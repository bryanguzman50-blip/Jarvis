# FAQ — Standard Answers

*Answers Jarvis uses when drafting replies to sellers and buyers. Edit to match how Bryan actually answers; add every new question that comes up twice.*

## From sellers

**"Are you actually going to buy my house?"**
I work with a network of local buyers. Either I buy it or one of my partners does — either way you get a cash closing on your timeline and you don't pay agent commissions or make repairs.

**"How did you get my number/address?"**
Public records — I look for properties where the owner might benefit from a quick, as-is sale. If you'd rather not hear from me again, say the word and you won't.

**"How fast can you close?"**
Typically 2–3 weeks, sometimes faster. You pick the date.

**"Is your offer negotiable?"**
The offer reflects the numbers — repairs, resale value, and my costs. If you think I've got the repair estimate wrong, walk me through it and I'll re-run it.

## From buyers

**"What's your assignment fee?"**
Priced into the number you see. The spread is the spread — if the deal works at my price, the fee shouldn't matter.
